06 / FINDINGS

Experimental observations

  • ArcFace achieved the highest correct known identification rate.
  • ArcFace achieved the highest unknown identity rejection rate.
  • FaceNet recorded the lowest average processing time per image.
  • dlib had the smallest recognition model weight file.
  • ArcFace had the highest measured processing time in this CPU experiment.
07 / RESEARCH NOTES

Methodology and limitations

Dataset protocol:
LFW was originally designed for face verification. This experiment uses a custom open-set identification protocol with a gallery of enrolled identities and separate query images.

Evaluation:
Thresholds were independently selected on a calibration subset of 4,870 images. Final comparison metrics use the held-out subset of 5,904 images.

Limitations:
These results are not official LFW verification benchmark scores. Processing times represent the tested software pipelines rather than isolated neural-network inference. This experiment does not establish suitability for real-world law-enforcement identification.
