↕Backup & RestoreDownload a full backup or restore saved data⌄

A full backup includes the SQLite recognition history database, registered face photos and processed face encodings. Stop the webcam before creating or restoring a backup. Store the ZIP somewhere private because it contains biometric data.

Restore from backup

Restore replaces the current history database, registered photos and encodings. Make a fresh backup of the current data first. The restore is disabled while the webcam is running.

⌫Clear Recognition HistoryRemove events for a selected date or clear all history⌄

Choose a date to delete only the recognition events recorded on that day. Registered people, their photos, face encodings and System Management activity records will remain unchanged. Stop the webcam before deleting history.

To remove every recognition event regardless of date, use the separate option below. This cannot be undone unless you have a backup.
